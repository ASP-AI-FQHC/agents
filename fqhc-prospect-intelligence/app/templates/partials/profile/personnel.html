What each source is worth

Form 990 rows come from a signed federal filing and describe the tax year shown; the form lists officers care of the organization’s own address, so it carries no direct contact details. HRSA UDS is the contact the health center itself gave its funder — a named person and a direct line. Website rows were read from a public page by a heuristic that can be wrong; treat them as leads to confirm, and the source page is linked on each line. Emails appear only where the organization published them itself.
