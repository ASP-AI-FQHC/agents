Executive Overview

Any FQHC operates in a demanding environment where reliability, security, and cost control are essential to delivering safe, continuous care across every site it serves. As a Federally Qualified Health Center, dependable and secure technology is no longer optional—it is foundational to clinical operations, HIPAA compliance, and patient trust.

ALLSTAR Partners proposes a structured, predictive monthly billing model designed to eliminate the frustration and uncertainty of variable IT expenses. This approach replaces reactive, project-based invoicing with a consistent monthly investment that covers a broad range of technical support and security services. Any FQHC gains clear financial visibility while ensuring continuous access to experienced professionals who understand the operational demands of multi-site healthcare environments.

ALLSTAR Partners brings a proven track record of delivering reliable, high-value managed services to organizations that demand accountability, security, and consistency.

Our philosophy is simple: build stability, bake in security, and provide leadership—not just support. This proposal reflects a long-term partnership focused on operational confidence, financial predictability, and measurable results.

Proposal Summary

Any FQHC (referred to as “Client”) delivers healthcare services to its community. ALLSTAR Partners (referred to as “ASP”) is a Cybersecurity-focused Managed Service Provider (MSP+) with extensive experience in supporting organizations like Any FQHC with their technology requirements. Any FQHC has requested a proposal from ASP to provide the following technology services:

ASP Secure IT ® Managed Site

The foundational hub for all our services. From this core, Managed Services deliver vulnerability monitoring, proactive network log collection, strategic IT reporting, and IT recommendations for your business.

ASP Secure IT ® Managed User

Ongoing cybersecurity training, including phishing attack simulations, dark web monitoring, enforcement of security policies, and interactive leaderboards to track progress and foster a security-conscious culture.

ASP Secure IT ® Managed Identity

24/7 SOC monitoring and ITDR for session hijacking, credential theft, suspicious inbox rules, and rogue apps, with investigations, triage, and threat hunting. Automated remediation isolates identities. Daily offsite backups of email, files, and data enable fast recovery. ISPM hardens identity posture.

ASP Secure IT ® Managed Device

24/7 SOC monitoring of traffic on all segments via SIEM appliances, with alert triage and appliance management. Activity monitoring covers device logs from configured sources. Device Support includes lifecycle management, remediation, and Direct-to-Cloud config backups.

ASP Secure IT ® Managed Workstation

Dual SOC monitoring with SentinelOne and Huntress for 24/7 oversight of computers with MDR agents, with alert triage and EDR agent management. Remediation delivers 24/7 threat containment with custom reports. DNS Filtering manages content filtering and threat reporting.

ASP Secure IT ® Managed Server

24/7 NOC monitoring of servers with RMM agents and dual SOC monitoring via SentinelOne & Huntress, with alert triage and agent management. Remediation delivers 24/7 threat containment with custom reports. DNS Filtering manages content filtering and threat reporting.
