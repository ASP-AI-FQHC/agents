# SSHCO Partners - AI-Powered Healthcare Solutions

## Project Overview

SSHCO Partners is a comprehensive website showcasing innovative AI healthcare solutions designed specifically for South Side Chicago communities. The platform presents 8 distinct AI agents that complement clinical teams and empower residents with accessible, culturally-responsive healthcare support.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- **Community-Centered Healthcare**: Deploy AI solutions that respect and enhance community health initiatives
- **Cultural Competency**: Provide multilingual, culturally-aware healthcare support
- **Accessibility First**: Ensure 24/7 healthcare assistance through familiar channels (SMS, voice, WhatsApp)
- **Clinical Complement**: Enhance, never replace, human healthcare providers
- **Privacy by Design**: Implement secure, HIPAA-compliant AI solutions
